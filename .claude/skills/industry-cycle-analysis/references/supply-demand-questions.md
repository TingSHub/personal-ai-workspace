# Supply-Demand Questions

Use these questions to turn an industry into a concrete chain of conflicts.

## Chain Questions

- What is the end demand?
- Which downstream product converts that demand into orders?
- Which midstream product is the first measurable bottleneck?
- Which upstream material, equipment, or process limits expansion?
- Which node has the longest lead time?
- Which node has the lowest substitutability?

## Demand Questions

- Is demand real consumption, capital expenditure, inventory restocking, policy-driven demand, or expectation-driven demand?
- Is demand one-time or recurring?
- Is demand domestic, overseas, or global?
- Is demand concentrated in a few customers?
- Is the customer willing to pay higher prices?
- Is the buyer funding the budget from operating cash flow, balance-sheet cash, debt, equity, or public funding? What observable condition would tighten or cancel that budget?
- Is the disclosed backlog supported by deposits, binding long-term or take-or-pay terms, ordinary cancellable orders, or only framework intentions?
- Could customers be placing duplicate or multi-supplier orders to reserve scarce capacity? What shipment, inventory, cancellation, or customer-use data would disprove that?
- What data confirms demand: shipment, order, capex, utilization, tender, price, or import/export?

## Supply Questions

- Who currently has capacity?
- What is nominal capacity vs effective capacity?
- What is utilization?
- What is yield?
- What capacity is under construction?
- What is the ramp-up schedule?
- What equipment, material, labor, customer qualification, or regulation blocks expansion?
- Can competitors substitute the constrained node?

## Shortage Questions

- What exactly is short?
- How much is short?
- How long will it be short?
- Who benefits from the shortage?
- Who is harmed by the shortage?
- Is the shortage visible in price, delivery time, order backlog, inventory, or margin?
- Is the shortage already known by the market?

## Expansion Questions

- Who is expanding?
- Is the disclosed capex cash paid, asset additions, finance leases or guidance, and does it include maintenance, offices, land, software, logistics or other non-capacity items?
- How much is growth/expansion capex versus maintenance or replacement capex? If undisclosed, is the mix explicitly marked as a gap?
- How much is planned?
- How much is funded?
- How much is actually under construction?
- When does it enter trial production?
- When does it become effective capacity?
- What utilization rate is needed to avoid losses?
- Will expansion solve the shortage or create oversupply?
